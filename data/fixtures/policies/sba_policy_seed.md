# SBA 7(a) Underwriting Policy — Seed Document

## INCOME-004 — Allowable Income Sources
Cryptocurrency mining income, NFT sales proceeds, and unverified digital asset income are **not** allowable income sources for SBA 7(a) qualification unless seasoned for 24 months with full documentation.

## SBA-REV-002 — Revenue Reconciliation
Stated annual gross revenues on SBA Form 1919 must reconcile within **10%** of tax returns, IRS transcripts (4506-C), and profit & loss statements. Material discrepancies require manual review.

## SBA-ENTITY-001 — Business Existence
The applicant business must be verifiable through state registry records. Shell companies operating from virtual offices require enhanced due diligence.

## SBA-EMP-003 — Employment Verification
Employer named on the loan application must match third-party employment verification letters. Mismatches are treated as potential fraud indicators.

## GENERAL-001 — Default Review Threshold
Any high-severity fraud indicator automatically routes to manual underwriting review. Auto-decline is disabled in advisory mode.
