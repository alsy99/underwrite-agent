# Specialty Mortgage — Bank Statement Underwriting Policy

## BSM-INC-001 — Qualifying Deposit Income
Stated monthly income on the loan application must reconcile within **15%** of average eligible deposits on the submitted bank statements (typically 12–24 months). Material gaps require a letter of explanation and manual review.

## BSM-DEP-002 — Large Deposit Scrutiny
Non-payroll deposits above **$5,000** must be sourced. Unsourced large deposits are excluded from qualifying income and may indicate fraud or undisclosed liabilities.

## BSM-EMP-001 — Employment Consistency
Employer named on the application must match employment verification letters. Mismatches are treated as potential misrepresentation.

## BSM-ADDR-001 — Residential Occupancy
Primary residence address on the application should be consistent across statements and employment letters. Virtual mailbox / commercial suite residential claims require enhanced due diligence.

## GENERAL-001 — Default Review Threshold
Any high-severity fraud indicator automatically routes to manual underwriting review. Auto-decline requires multiple independent high-severity signals.
