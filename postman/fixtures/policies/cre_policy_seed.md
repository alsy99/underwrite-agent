# CRE Acquisition Underwriting Policy — Seed

## CRE-NOI-001 — Rent Roll Reconciliation
Net operating income on the rent roll must reconcile with executed lease terms within 5% variance.

## CRE-GUAR-002 — Guarantor Alignment
Lease guarantor entities must match operating agreement members or documented affiliate structures.

## CRE-ADDR-003 — Property Verification
Property address on appraisal must match lease and rent roll. Virtual office addresses require enhanced review.
